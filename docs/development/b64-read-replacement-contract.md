It retains that review's entire scope record, actual consumed completion, context and job scope. The native parser cross-checks those records and rejects another proposal, branching raw roots, or a changed origin. Its owner handoff is an ordinary user-initiated `read_revision_instruction` with **no completion**. The earlier review's genuine completion is provenance, not permission to acknowledge a new model response. Capture records exact raw words without confirmation or scope authority.

Raw replacements use their own predecessor chain. A new actual model result becomes a `review` successor of the original grouped review, not the raw question; therefore the new review can cite the latest genuine raw testimony without falsely retiring it. Blank/cancel remains pending; changed context, failed save or uncertain acknowledgment prevents egress. Returned mappings still require actual compiler analysis and a separately displayed grouped default-No decision.
