## Observed problem

A complete Read reply could supply an invented quote for a relevant field.
The producer's routing depended on labels and model-supplied evidence even
when the current attached policy already stated the field's task meaning.
An ordinary owner answer did not require another exact quote, yet the model
could still fail that representation step. In the independent installed
diagnostic, earlier completed Read responses also remained uncheckpointed
when the final question was saved. The ordinary restart guard correctly
refused that unresolved history before continuation.

The mini's invalid replies and terminal outcome are retained. This forward
repair does not infer recovery of its historical job, acknowledge an unknown
transfer, or convert a failed model sample into success.

## Bounded change

The Read producer associates an exact permitted resource/field and requested
use with the current approved policy or attributed operator context. The
association retains the complete Source digest/version and exact line text,
or the exact answer to the same current question. Model quotes remain
strictly verbatim. A model may omit a redundant quote when the host already
has the exact association; a fabricated quote still fails.

Explicit statements and task transitions are recognized conservatively.
Ambiguous identifiers, caller-input descriptions, enum lists, mere mentions
and unknown phrasing do not establish a stored field's meaning. Conflicting
explicit definitions require a new attributed interpretation, with the
original statements retained. These checks establish provenance and a
finite naming association, not general English entailment or complete
business-policy coverage.

A relevant missing meaning reaches the existing durable plain-language
question mechanism before another generation is spent merely asking it.
Generated field labels do not answer the question. Exact positive read
instructions remain requirements if a model omits their fields; the producer
does not add fields or invent a substitute operation.

An explicit nonanswer such as “I don't know” remains pending. Its exact text,
target, context, local-operator attribution and actual time are retained
before handling is acknowledged. Unknown attempts and usable answers share
the existing four-answer budget. Restart and cancellation do not reset it.
Exhaustion preserves the question and reports the limit without another
answer or generation. A later accepted Read retains the nonanswer history as
audit metadata.

Only successful response objects actually consumed by the bounded Read
producer can associate its preceding complete turns with the final typed
question checkpoint. The private response/completion identity and ordinary
coordinated save are still required. The generic checkpoint/restart fence
does not promote historical, partial, uncertain, unrelated or fabricated
responses. The existing owner handoff and compare-and-swap persistence
remain authoritative.

## Preserved boundaries

Meaning evidence grants no visibility, runtime identity, approval, Action
authority, database guarantee, observed effect or business readiness. No
private row query supplies question wording. Source, schema, catalog and
permission currentness are rechecked before saving or sending. Known failed
saves and uncertain acknowledgements stop further handling; competing
decisions cannot silently replace history.

No provider/model settings, correction limits, output caps, privacy filters,
Read grants, business rules, runtime execution or frozen installations are
changed. The Action producer keeps its existing behavior; only a new explicit
Read completion-association call uses the shared recovery mechanism.
