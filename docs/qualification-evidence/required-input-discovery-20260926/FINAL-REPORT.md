Exact ID, name/alias, phrase and all-term matches rank ahead of explicitly labelled partial matches, with stable ties and bounded work. Incidental grammatical terms may be ignored; business opposites remain meaningful. Partial matching requires a strong reviewed title/alias anchor, sufficient meaningful-term coverage and matching numeric terms. Selective posting-list intersections precede bounded fallback union. There is no indiscriminate OR search, private predicate/value index, vector store or case-specific synonym.

Catalog pages expose page item count, exact authorized matched total, continuation and end. Replay is deterministic within the same current session/query/limit/scope/revision/TTL and remains budget-accounted. Tampered or stale continuations are refused; publication, withdrawal and access changes invalidate navigation. Counts and ranking are computed after visibility filtering.

Compact Action cards describe all public companion effects, required caller inputs and the exact-detail route. Exact detail connects through current authorized Read projections to a usable root, a bounded subset of permitted identifying fields/operators and existing row-indexed opaque references. External, unavailable, ambiguous, relationship-only and unproven reference paths remain explicit. Templates are labelled templates; placeholders are unusable values. Input provenance comes from checked canonical metadata, not an equality-to-host-fact heuristic. Omitted approval conditions still establish neither manual-only policy, eligibility, automatic approval nor execution readiness.

It does not add COUNT or private aggregates, or promise a database snapshot across calls. A limited rows result does not certify complete private eligibility. The four tools and canonical input validators remain intact. Typed additive output metadata, final serialization, actual tools/list and existing outgoing-conformance checks are exercised.
