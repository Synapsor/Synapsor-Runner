# Complete authoring programs

Guided authoring can retain up to 64 selected Action intents in one saved program. It drafts one Action at a time, shows progress in recommended groups of eight, and saves the full agenda, original job, source attachment, scoped owner decisions and exact checkpoints. A work-unit boundary does not grant authority or establish completion. Canonical Action authority still supports its separate 4096-rule limit; existing per-Action, correction, source and provider limits are unchanged.

Programs larger than the legacy eight-intent agenda use `synapsor.boundary-authoring-resume.v2`. Restart and late edits preserve the complete agenda. Requested edits, removals and additions remain pending until their existing checked producers finish. A v2 program cannot publish a prepared subset as completion: the selected intents must have distinct current outcomes, exact current Read dependencies and current full-candidate attached-source coverage. Shared constraints and obligations remain part of the complete candidate. Legacy v1 subset-review semantics remain available.

Operator review shows eight outcomes per page, supports outcome-name search and exact outcome details, and retains the complete authority and immutable digest for the separate default-No confirmation. Active execution inventory has its own pages, including outcomes removed by a pending replacement. Viewing a page grants nothing; writer setup and execution permission remain separate reviews.

The documented structured export/import route remains disabled-only. Existing submissions of up to 12 added or changed Actions remain supported. A complete export with more than 12 entries uses `synapsor.boundary-draft-input.v2`; unchanged entries do not consume the 12-change allowance. The complete `actions` array contains the typed contracts. In v2, skeletons reference one complete `action_skeleton_candidates` catalog and exact contracts through local `/actions/<index>` references, instead of repeating their payloads. Those references are checked navigation metadata, never Action authority. Foreign, missing, wrong or stale references are refused; an edited contract is still independently compiled against host-loaded current records.

Exports keep the existing 512 KiB bound. A program that cannot fit is refused with its saved state intact; it is never truncated or called complete. Exact authority review remains available for higher-capacity canonical authority. Full source context and unknown-relevance requirements are not replaced with summaries or digests to fit a model request. Unresolved source or external duties remain pending, and model replies do not become owner facts.
