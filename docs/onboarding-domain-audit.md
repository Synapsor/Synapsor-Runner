## Qualification Rules

Freeze the executable before choosing two previously unused policies/layouts,
including one structurally different layout. A renamed/coded equivalent is an
additional regression, not a substitute. Use new owned disposable databases and
the public setup/client path, not saved authority or injected draft JSON.

For each case record complete setup/edit time, business and permission decisions,
repeats, retries, help, editor detours and administrator/developer assistance.
Demonstrate eligible execution with receipt/readback, human review, refusal,
same-client rule changes, add/remove behavior, pending-request handling, privacy
and concurrency. Preserve failed runs when changing code; freeze again and add
another unseen case. A successful assisted test is not unaided onboarding.

Current limits and original-domain evidence remain in
[Action Capability Qualification](action-capability-qualification.md).
